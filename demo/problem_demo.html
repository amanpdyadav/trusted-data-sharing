Names are the least of it

With names removed, a few clues still single out 1 of 46,398 visits in both cases: time, town, sex, age and injury type. Quasi-identifiers must be protected together.

Measure against what attackers know

A post, a news clip, a phone directory and an old leak were enough to start. Our tool must replay attacks like these and show every candidate set holding 5 or more people.

Every release, its own protection

Once a name is known, any unprotected dataset opens up. Each release needs its own plan and its own pseudonym key, so releases can’t be joined.
