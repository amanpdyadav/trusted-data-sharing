From one social post to a full profile

A new page in our Streamlit app (From one post). It starts from a single public post and follows the person through every dataset, released the usual way with names, ID codes, phones and street addresses removed. Then it runs the same attack on our HEALTH-S1 release. All people are synthetic.

For the demo video: the page opens in presenter mode. Each click on Next reveals one clue. A big counter shows how many patients still fit, the "what the attacker knows" panel fills up, and a link graph lights up each dataset as it is joined. The last step replays the same post against our release. The second tab, Explore any post, has the full funnels and link table for all 948 posts.

How it works

  • Clues from the post: time posted, profile city, display name, and a symptom-to-diagnosis-group keyword map in Finnish, English and Swedish.
  • Sex from first name: the most common sex for that first name, a stand-in for public name statistics.
  • Public directory: a full name gives the postcode. Otherwise first name + postcode gives the name, which is sometimes wrong. The app says so when it is.
  • Pivot: birth date + postcode joins the bank, energy, transport (plus trip history and most-used stops) and school releases.
  • Code: pets/reid_chain.py (logic, survey() runs all posts in about 3 s) and app/pages/2_From_one_post.py.

Limits to state in the demo

  • The symptom keyword map was checked against the data, so it is generous to the attacker. That makes the result a fair worst case.
  • Customer service, permits, smart city and cyber data have no birth date, so this chain can't reach them.
  • Even on our release, 199 visits is a small pool. It is still above k = 5, and nothing in it joins to other data.
