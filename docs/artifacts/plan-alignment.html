How close are they?

About 70%. Same thesis, same dataset and the same "one scrap → full profile" arc. The lead's page is stronger on storytelling (two cases, more datasets, masked IDs, raw data). Ours is stronger on proof: it covers all 948 posts and runs the attack on our own protected output. Together they make the video's first shot: their chain, then "Press Protect" with our numbers.

  • Gap in ours: we don't read "Spent 3 hours" as length of stay, and our first-name list misses names like Riitta. So case 1 stops at 2 candidates in our chain, while the demo reaches 1. Both are small fixes.
  • Gap in theirs: it never shows a release that holds. The quickstart is "names removed" in their framing, and the page has no "after" from our tool.
