What TrustED is building

A read of the TrustED project's website: what they already have, what they are piloting, and what that means for how we pitch the PETs Transformer.

EU Horizon Europe · grant 101168467 Led by Gradiant · 10 partners, 5 countries CORDIS Project of the Month, Sep 2026

In one paragraph

TrustED is an EU research project on trustworthy European data spaces. Its two pillars are self-sovereign digital identity (people hold verifiable credentials in a wallet and share only what is needed) and privacy-preserving technologies that let organisations use data together without handing it over. Its website centres on four themes: data minimisation, federated learning for health, secure aggregation and tests with real users.

What they care about

Data minimisation

Their September article argues against “all-or-nothing” sharing: share strictly what the purpose needs (GDPR Art. 5(1)(c)). This is exactly what a scenario-aware PET plan does for a dataset.

Health data across institutions

Their flagship health pilot is multi-hospital respiratory-infection prediction. Our health dataset has an influenza-like-illness dashboard scenario (HEALTH-S2).

Aggregates over raw data

The SASS article says many analyses need only aggregates. Secret sharing plus differential privacy is their recommended combination.

Real people, not cryptographers

Their pilots test whether non-experts understand and trust the tools. Plain-language explanations and consent are part of the goal.

Transparency and audit

eIDAS2 principles they highlight include granular consent, revocation and transaction logs that show what was shared, with whom and when.

EU regulation timeline

EUDI Wallets are mandatory for public administrations from late 2026 and for banks, energy, transport and health in 2027. Compliance framing resonates.
